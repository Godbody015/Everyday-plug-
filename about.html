ABOUT EVERYDAY PLUG

Helping businesses get moving.

We help local and growing businesses establish their digital presence, access practical technology and connect with the right people to bring their ideas to life.

OUR STORY

Making digital solutions easier to access.

Starting a business today often means dealing with websites, social media, branding, technology, business systems and many other digital requirements.

For many entrepreneurs, knowing what they need is difficult enough — finding the right people to help can be even harder.

Everyday Plug exists to simplify that process.

We help businesses understand what they need, identify practical solutions and coordinate the work required to get those solutions in place.

WHAT WE DO

More than just building websites.

Our services are focused on helping businesses establish themselves, operate more effectively and grow digitally.

🌐

Digital Presence

Websites, domains, hosting and online platforms that help businesses establish themselves online.

📈

Business Growth

Digital marketing and social media solutions that help businesses reach and communicate with customers.

🎨

Branding

Practical branding and graphic design solutions that help businesses present themselves professionally.

⚙️

Business Technology

POS systems, merchant platforms and other digital tools that can help businesses operate more effectively.

HOW WE WORK

The right solution starts with understanding the problem.

We don't believe every business needs the same solution. Our approach starts with understanding what you're trying to achieve.

01

Understand

02

03

OUR NETWORK

The right people for the right job.

Some projects require specialist skills or services. When they do, Everyday Plug can work with trusted professionals and technology partners to help deliver what the client needs.

This allows us to remain focused on understanding the client's needs and finding a practical path forward.

OUR MISSION

Make digital solutions more accessible.

We want entrepreneurs to have easier access to the technology, services and support they need to establish and grow their businesses.

OUR VISION

Become a trusted digital business partner.

Our vision is to grow Everyday Plug into a trusted digital business partner for entrepreneurs across South Africa.

A place where businesses can find practical technology, marketing, education and digital solutions — and the people who can help bring them to life.
