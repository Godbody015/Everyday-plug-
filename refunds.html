Refunds & Cancellations

General information regarding project cancellations and payment-related matters.

1. Project Agreements

Payment, cancellation, refund and delivery conditions may differ depending on the service and project agreed with the client.

2. Before Work Begins

Where a project has not yet started, cancellation arrangements will depend on the specific agreement between Everyday Plug and the client.

3. Work Already Completed

Where work has already been completed or specialist resources have already been committed to a project, the applicable payment and cancellation conditions will depend on the agreed project terms.

4. Third-Party Costs

Domain registrations, hosting, software subscriptions, third-party services and specialist services may have separate fees and cancellation policies.

5. Contact

If you have a question about a specific project, please contact Everyday Plug before making a payment or requesting cancellation.
